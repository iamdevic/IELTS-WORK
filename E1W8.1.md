The diagram illustrates various stages involved in production and processing of milk and dairy products.

Overall, the process has eight stages which begin with a herd of cows grazing on grass and end with the goods reaching commercial stores. The production and processing phase is linear and largely automated by machines.

The initial step is to let the cows graze on green pastures. Then, the cows are taken to a milking facility, where they are milked via milking machines twice a day. Next, the collected milk is stored in large refrigerated storage tanks. Following that, the milk is transported to the dairy for further processing through milk tankers. Every day, the procedures for gathering, storing, and transporting to dairy plants are repeated.

In subsequent stages, some of the milk is pasteurized and packed into bottles. Additionally, the remaining milk is processed in dairy to produce dairy products such as cheese, cream and butter. Finally, the finished goods are delivered to shelves of supermarkets and shops, ending the process.
