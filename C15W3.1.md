The diagram illustrates the process by which noodles are produced.

Overall, Manufacturing process consists of eight stages, beginning with pumping flour into silos and ending with labelling and sealing of finished noodles cups. The process is largely automated and involves several stages: mixing, rolling, cutting, molding, cooking and packaging.

The process starts when flour is pumped to large silos by a truck. After storage of flour, It is transferred to a mixer, where it is combined with oil and water to form dough. Next, The dough is kneaded by several rollers to prepare thin sheets. Then, these thin sheets are cutted by a machine, which has many blades, to form dough strips.

In subsequent stages, These noodles strips are molded into noodles discs. After that they are cooked with oil and left to dry. Next, The processed discs are then placed into cups along with vegetables and spices. At the last step, the process ends when the cups are labelled and sealed.
